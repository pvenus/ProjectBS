# Golden Fang visual selection

- Selected: candidate C.
- C has the clearest single low curved canine silhouette, restrained peeling brass patch, cracked root, and worn leather tie. It reads as a humble merchant charm rather than jewelry or a legendary trophy.
- A rejected: bulky vertical form weakens the 32px curved-fang silhouette.
- B rejected: split root grooves risk reading as a bundle of teeth/horn slats.
- RGB, geometry, brass coverage, and tie placement are fixed after selection.
